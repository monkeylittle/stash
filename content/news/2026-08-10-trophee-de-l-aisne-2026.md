I was thrilled to finish second overall at the 2026 Trophée de l’Aisne, a three-day stage race in France from 8 to 10 August, in the U15 and U17 girls’ category. I finished just four points behind the winner, and it was a great way to follow up on my second place last year.

Stage one, on Saturday 8 August, was held on the velodrome at Saint-Quentin, with a 200 m time trial and a pursuit. I was second in both, which put me second on the stage and in the overall standings going into the road stages.

Sunday’s stage two, at Saint-Paul-aux-Bois, was my day. I won every prime and I won the stage. I also bridged across to a women’s field that had started five minutes ahead of us, and rode past them. I was delighted with how it went.

On the final day, Monday 10 August, I finished second again on stage three, at Frières-Faillouel. That left me second overall behind a very strong rider, with a stage win and two second places to show for the weekend.

Thank you to everyone who has supported me, and to my partners and supporters for making racing abroad like this possible.
