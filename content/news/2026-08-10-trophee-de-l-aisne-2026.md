I was back at the Trophée de l'Aisne in France, and I came home second overall again. This time I was just four points off the winner. It was a great way to follow up on my second place last year.

The three-day stage race ran from 8 to 10 August 2026, in the U15 and U17 girls' category. It started on the Saturday on the velodrome at Saint-Quentin, with a 200 m time trial and a pursuit. I was second in both, so I went into the road stages second on the stage and in the overall standings.

Sunday's stage two, at Saint-Paul-aux-Bois, was my day. I won every prime and I won the stage. I did not really plan to bridge across to the women's field, which had started five minutes ahead of us. It just happened. Zélie Camboulives, who finished second, and I worked brilliantly together. She was strong and committed, and we rode past the women's field. It was great fun racing that hard.

On the final day, Monday 10 August, stage three was at Frières-Faillouel. I crossed the line first, but Zélie had won the intermediate sprints, so she took the stage on points. The overall is decided on those points, not on stage wins. That left me second overall, four points behind Zélie.

She is a fantastic rider, and I really enjoyed racing against her. Two stage wins and a second place overall was a great way to end the weekend.

Thank you to everyone who has supported me, and to my partners and supporters for making racing abroad like this possible.
