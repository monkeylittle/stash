I am so excited, and so proud, to have been selected to represent Ireland at the European Youth Olympic Festival (EYOF) in North Macedonia, from 20 to 25 July 2025. Pulling on the Ireland jersey is something I have dreamed about for a long time, and it still doesn't quite feel real.

At our media day, the team came together and we were photographed in our Ireland kit, with our bikes and wheels and with the flag. It gave all of us a real taste of what it must be like to be a professional athlete. Seeing how much goes on behind the scenes before a major games made it feel more real than ever. It also made me even more determined to do the jersey justice.

I'll be lining up for the road events at the festival, racing the individual time trial on 22 July and the road race on 24 July. I can't wait to race against the best young riders in Europe, soak up the whole experience and give it everything I have.

Thank you to everyone who has helped me get here, and to my partners and supporters for making it possible. I'll share more as the festival gets closer.
