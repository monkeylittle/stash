If you like cyclo-cross, you want a day like this one. Round 3 of the National Cyclo-Cross Series was hosted by De Ronde at the Cork Showgrounds in Curraheen, and it was properly muddy. I had a great time.

It was the kind of mud that makes you swap bikes every lap. That meant a quick dash into the pits, a fresh bike and straight back out again. The pits were a bit chaotic, with lots of people in the pit lane, which made the bike changes tricky. It is hard work, but I love it. It is half the fun of cyclo-cross.

I was racing in the Junior category, in the same race as the Senior and Master women, so there was plenty of company on the course. Four laps later, I had somehow taken the win, 3 minutes 4 seconds clear of second place. My first lap was my quickest, and after that it was all about staying upright and getting through the mud.

I finished in 46 minutes 17 seconds, and I was delighted with that on a day like that one.

A big thank you to De Ronde and the volunteers for putting on the race in those conditions. Thank you too to everyone who helped me get there, and to my partners and supporters for making it possible.
