I was surprised and delighted to finish second overall at the Trophée de l’Aisne, a three-day road stage race in France from 12 to 14 July 2025, racing as a guest rider for NXTG.

I have admired NXTG from afar for a long time. I have watched how they conduct themselves at races, with such professionalism, teamwork and respect for each other and for everyone around them, and it is exactly the kind of team I want to learn from. So when the chance came to pull on their kit, I was really excited. I am so grateful to Natasha for making it happen.

Racing in the NXTG colours was a brilliant experience. I really appreciated the opportunity to race alongside Imke, Maxime and Jaylinn, under the management of Marco, and to see up close how they prepare, work together and look after one another. Being part of that, even for a few days, taught me a huge amount.

The racing itself went better than I could have hoped. Stage one was held on an outdoor velodrome, which doesn’t really play to my strengths, so I was pleased with fifth place. Stage two suited me much better. It had a fast, technical descent, then a prime, then a brutal climb that blew the race to pieces. I loved it, and finished third on the stage. On the final day I won stage three with a long breakaway, finishing clear of the field. That secured second place in the general classification. The girls were brilliant and it was a real team result. Winning a stage in those colours, with a team who believed in me, is a moment I will not forget.

Thank you to NXTG for the invitation, to Natasha, Marco and the whole team for making me feel so welcome, and to my partners and supporters, who make racing abroad like this possible.
