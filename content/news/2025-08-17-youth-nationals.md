What a weekend! I came home from the Youth Road Nationals in Co. Cork as the U15 national champion in both the time trial and the road race. I added a silver medal in the criterium too.

The championships were on 16 and 17 August 2025. They were hosted by Sliabh Luachra Cycling Club and Fermoy Cycling Club, and I was racing in the colours of Orwell Wheelers.

It started on the Saturday in Knocknagree, with the 8.46 km time trial. It was tight. I finished in 14 minutes 20.94 seconds and won by just 1.85 seconds. Every second counted. Standing on the top step of the podium was a brilliant feeling.

There was hardly time to enjoy it, because the road race followed later that day. It was 46.5 km, and the U15 and U16 girls raced together for separate titles. I knew from previous races which riders were likely to work together. So when some of the U16 riders attacked the bunch, I did not chase them. It made the race simpler. Two of them stayed away and finished ahead of me, and I could focus on the U15 race. Four of us arrived at the finish on the same time, and I was the first U15 across the line. That made me U15 champion for the second time that day.

On the Sunday we moved to Fermoy for the criterium. It was another combined race with separate titles, and it was fast. I crossed the line third overall and second in the U15 category. That earned me a silver medal to go with the two titles.

Two national titles and a silver in one weekend is something I will remember for a long time. Thank you to everyone who has supported me, and to my partners and supporters for helping make it possible. I could not do it without you.
