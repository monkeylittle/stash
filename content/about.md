I’m Katie, a cyclist from Dublin, and in 2027 I’ll be racing for [Acrog-Tormans Balen BC](https://acrog-tormans.be), a youth cycling club in Mol, Belgium. I began my journey attending the Sunday morning youth sessions in Corkagh Park organised by my local club.  As I watched the older riders move from the park races to the road and I knew I wanted to follow in their footsteps. I’ve been chasing them ever since.

I race on the road, the track and in the field because each discipline teaches me something different. The road means tactics and endurance, the track means speed and positioning while cyclocross means grit, mud and a lot of fun. They all make me a better rider.

Racing is only part of it for me. I love getting out on the bike with friends, greenway rides and stopping for ice cream.  I want more girls to try cycling and see how friendly and supportive the racing community is.

Now I’m racing abroad, with my sights set on the world stage. I’m grateful to everyone who makes that possible, and I’d love you to come along for the ride.

## Highlights

- Represented Ireland at the European Youth Olympic Festival in North Macedonia
- Many national titles in road, track and cyclocross
- Consistent top-10 finishes at the Ronde van Vlaanderen and Gent-Wevelgem youth days
- Second overall, and 2 stage wins at the Trophée de l’Aisne in France, July 2026
